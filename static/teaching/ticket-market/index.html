The scramble

No market price. Just fans bumping into each other.

  • Everyone wanders the room. When a ticket holder bumps into someone who values the ticket more, they trade, at a price halfway between their two values.
  • Nobody sets a price, and nobody can see the whole room. Anyone can buy a ticket and later sell it on.
