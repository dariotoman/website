Who Pays a Tax?

The government collects a tax of t on every unit sold. Buyers now pay Pb, sellers keep Ps = Pb − t. Who actually bears the tax depends on how elastic each side is, not on who hands the money over. Move the sliders, or start from one of the markets in the lecture.
